# Photoreal Tech Explainer — video style skill

The premium tier of the dark explainer family: real ray-traced 3D. Everyday objects
(a tire, a brick, a key) become monumental under studio light; the camera pushes and
orbits slowly; annotation lines label the physics; a mascot reacts below; every visual
event has a sound. Feels like an Apple product film that teaches.

Generated by [Reverse Any Video](https://github.com/rahul-kumbhar0/Reverse-any-video)
on 2026-11-03 from a 55s photoreal reference. Spec: `style-spec.json`.

## The 5 rules that make this style (non-negotiable)

1. **Real 3D, really lit.** Hero shots are ray-traced (blender_shot.py): near-black world
   `#050507`, large soft key high-left, **amber rim (#E0A33E) aimed at the subject and
   pooling on the floor**, faint cool blue fill, AgX tone mapping, f/1.8 DOF.
2. **The camera never stops.** Every shot is a slow eased push-in (2–4%/s) or partial
   orbit; scene changes are morphs/pushes, ~4 hard cuts per minute max.
3. **The caption system** (same as mascot-tech-explainer): one top phrase, ≤5 words,
   dimmed gray + bold white + exactly one blue selection-boxed key word per beat.
4. **Annotation-line physics**: thin white lines with dot endpoints label parts of the 3D
   (measurements in small caps); tiny gray corner footnotes disclaim ("Magnified
   illustration"). Gauges/dials with moving needles are the evidence device.
5. **Everything sounds.** Whoosh on every morph, impact when parts meet, ticks when
   labels draw, riser into the reveal, low room tone under all of it (~78 events/min).

## Production recipe

1. Script to the formula (hook < 3s, mechanism, receipt, history, payoff, share-CTA);
   ~2.5 words/s budget.
2. For each hero beat write a `blender_shot.py` scene JSON: primitives/simple assemblies,
   materials per spec (`roughness` 0.25–0.35 plastic, 0.25 metallic 0.9 steel), the light
   rig verbatim (key area ~170W size 7; amber spot ~2600–3000W `aim_at` subject; cool spot
   ~400–700W), camera keys eased, `--fps-render 10-12 --samples 24-32 --scale 0.45-0.5`.
3. Composite in Remotion: hero MP4s as `<OffthreadVideo>`, caption band + annotation
   overlays + mascot (new character, crossfade poses ~150ms) on top, DOM scenes for
   evidence cards/share sheet.
4. SFX pass: `synth_sfx.py all`, place by the grammar, room tone bed, −22dB under VO.
5. Verify: extract frames, check lighting (floor pool visible? subject rim-lit?), captions
   (one boxed word), fidelity checklist below.

## Fidelity checklist

- [ ] Hero shots ray-traced; amber light pool visible on floor; background near-black
- [ ] Camera moving in every shot, eased; no static frame anywhere
- [ ] Hook claim readable by 3.0s; captions ≤5 words, exactly one selection-boxed word
- [ ] ≥1 annotation line + ≥1 corner footnote + 1 evidence receipt (patent/spec)
- [ ] Mascot bottom-center ~18%, pose changes ≥1×/5s, crossfaded ~150ms
- [ ] SFX on every morph/land/label; room tone bed; no dead air; ≥0.5s end silence
- [ ] CTA = share-sheet scene, not a text card

## Do NOT

- Clone the reference mascot (red tee + green sunglasses), the speedy_devv handle, or any
  reference sentence/footage. New mascot, new words, new objects.
- Use flat/even lighting or pure black #000 backgrounds — the near-black must still shade.
- Hard-cut as rhythm; let the camera and captions carry it.
- Use blue for anything but caption selection boxes.
